Sea of Åland:

Weakening south to south-easterly wind, at first 7-10 m/s, in the afternoon 2-6 m/s.
Gradually strenghtening wind from the evening, after midnight 4-8 m/s, before noon 8-13 m/s.
At first sleet or rain in some places.
In the evening and at night rain in some places, otherwise good visibility.
